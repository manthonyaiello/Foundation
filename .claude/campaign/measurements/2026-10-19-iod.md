Why the spec layer is harder, to analyze later: its recursions must be
proved one index at a time (the implementation's loops are unrolled in one
proof); its unconstrained results go through the secondary stack (GNAT's
runtime executed in SAW); its bit sequences (Boolean arrays, 128-bit index)
are bit-by-bit where the implementation works on bytes and words; null
ranges appear far more often in the spec's slicing, forcing lax arithmetic.
