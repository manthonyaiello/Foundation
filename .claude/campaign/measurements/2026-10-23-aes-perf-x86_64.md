# #51 AES size and performance, x86_64 Linux (the x86_64 instance): the raw reports

Two back-to-back runs of `spark/scripts/perf-aes.sh` at `6023e8a`, unmodified (its Linux path, untested before, ran as written), on an AWS c8i.2xlarge (Intel Xeon 6975P-C, 8 vCPUs, Ubuntu 24.04) with nothing else running. README "Performance" quotes run 1. The second's medians are within 3% of the first's in 86 of 87 cells and within 5.4% in all; the sizes are identical.

On Linux, GCC's LTO inlines `Expand_Key`, `Encrypt_Block`, `Decrypt_Block` and `Decrypt_Block_Prime` into the `Perf_AES` wrappers (on macOS the wrappers stayed 4-byte branches), so the "wrappers" column holds most of the SPARK AES and "AES text" alone is not comparable with hex's or with the Rust's: compare "AES total". `nm -S` lists no `.constprop`/`.isra`/`.part` clone of an AES symbol, so nothing is missed.
